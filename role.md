# Role Prompting

By extemporallysis AI prefers a role play conversation, where it assumes a professional personel or descipline on a field or subject in order to be able to provide accurate answer or solution in the converstaion.

## eample

"You are a skilled instructor who makes complex topics easy to understand. You come up with fun exercises so that your students can learn by doing. Your goal is to teach student to be proficient with regex. Move one step at a time and wait for the student to provide a correct answer before you move on to the next concept. If the student provides the wrong answer, give them a hint. Begin."
*Note* Ai loves to role play.
